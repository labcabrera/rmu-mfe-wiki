# What RMU Engine is and how to use it with the books

RMU Engine is a **table companion** for Rolemaster Unified. It keeps the bookkeeping that takes the most time at the table: character sheets, bonuses, development points, equipment and, above all, combat phase by phase.

It does not replace the books. The rules are in the books of Iron Crown Enterprises (ICE), and the tool is not affiliated with ICE. This wiki explains the mechanics in its own words and cites the section of the book with the full rule.

> References such as "Core Law §8.2" point to the section of the book with the full rule.

## 1. What it does and what it does not {#what}

| The app takes care of                                                 | You (or the GM) decide, with the book at hand    |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Skill, stat, race and profession bonuses                              | What a character does and why                    |
| Development points, ranks and levelling up                            | Which skills fit the character                   |
| Resolving attacks: OB, DB, parry, table lookup, criticals and fumbles | Describing what happens in the scene             |
| Hits, bleeding, stun, fatigue and conditions                          | Whatever the book leaves to the GM               |
| Ordering the round by initiative and phases, and the action points    | Where the miniatures stand on the board          |
| Warning when a rule calls for a check (fear, resistance, maneuver)    | Whether an optional rule or a house rule applies |

When the app and the book disagree, **the book wins**. A difference that is not explained as a house rule is a bug in the app.

## 2. The books and the app {#books}

The app has the game data it needs to calculate, but not the text of the rules. This is what you will find from each book:

| Book                    | What the app uses                                                                                                               | Where                                               |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| **Core Law**            | Races, cultures, professions, skills, talents, maneuvers, attack, critical and fumble tables, healing, fear and morale, poisons | **Core** menu, character sheet and combat dashboard |
| **Character Companion** | Additional cultures, professions, talents and fighting styles                                                                   | **Core** menu (talents show their source book)      |
| **Spell Law**           | Spell lists and spells, with translated names and descriptions                                                                  | **Spells** menu                                     |
| **Treasure Law**        | Magic items, materials, trading, alchemy and treasure tables                                                                    | **Items** menu and the campaign market              |
| **Creature Law**        | Creatures as NPCs and encounter tables                                                                                          | **NPCs** menu                                       |

You will need the books to understand **why** each thing happens, to build characters that make sense and for everything the app does not cover: magic outside combat, travel, the setting…

## 3. Account and book access {#access}

- **Without an account** you only see the home page and this wiki.
- **There is no open sign-up.** As the tool has no ICE licence, accounts are created beforehand. With an account you can create campaigns, characters and your own content.
- **Book access:** part of the content is only shown to whoever has activated the matching book. It is activated in **Profile → Add activation code**: you check the code and activate it. The profile shows what you have under **Enabled features**.

| Needs the book activated                                                                                                                                                                        | Still available without it                                                              |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Core Law:** the whole attack, critical and fumble tables; the healing, fear, morale and poison tables; the skill difficulty guides; the fighting styles catalog; materials and trading tables | Looking up **one roll** of a table, which is what combat does: attacks resolve the same |
| **Treasure Law:** the treasure and alchemy tables                                                                                                                                               | The rest of the items and the market                                                    |

Without access, the page says so ("The whole tables need Core Law access") or shows only the explanation, without the table.

## 4. How it is organised {#modules}

| Menu          | What for                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Core**      | The rules as a catalog: realms, races, cultures, professions, skills, talents, fighting styles, and tools to roll maneuvers and resistances and look up the attack tables |
| **Campaigns** | Strategic games: the campaign, its factions and characters, with the calendar, experience and economy                                                                     |
| **Games**     | Tactical games: the fights, round by round, on the combat dashboard                                                                                                       |
| **NPCs**      | Non-player characters and creatures, and encounters                                                                                                                       |
| **Items**     | The item catalog and the treasure tables                                                                                                                                  |
| **Spells**    | Spell lists and spells                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Wiki**      | These guides. They need no account                                                                                                                                        |

The [first game guide](first-game.md) walks through these menus in order, from the campaign to the first fight.

## 5. Rulebook content and your own content {#homebrew}

Everything the app ships with has the source **Rulebook**; what you create, **User**. Only an administrator changes rulebook content, but you can create your own and mix it in.

- **The realm** is the world of the campaign: its races, languages, regions, religions and magic presence. You can use one that ships with the app or create your own.
  - What you create inside a realm (races, items, NPCs) follows that realm's permissions.
  - A realm is **shared** with friends, read or write, with the **Share** button.
- **Races:** you can **copy a race** from the rulebook into your realm and adjust it.
- **NPCs and creatures:** catalog ones cannot be changed, but you can **duplicate them as your own NPC** and edit the copy.
- **Cultures, professions, talents, skills, items, spell lists and spells:** created from their menu page with the create button.
- **Visibility:** your content is private unless you mark it public or share it.

If you write your own descriptions, use your own words: do not copy the text of the books.

## 6. Using it at the table {#at-the-table}

- **One device for the GM** with the combat dashboard open (a tablet or a laptop; it does not fit on a phone) and, if you like, the players with their sheet on theirs.
- **The dice stay on the table.** Every roll has a field to type the result of the physical dice. If you leave it empty, the app rolls for you in many cases.
- **The (i) icon** next to a value explains where it comes from: the modifiers added up, the rule that applies and the section of the book. It is the quick way to settle a "why do I get this?".
- **Campaign options:** stat generation, power level, lethality, board scale and others are set when the campaign is created. Each has its help with the default value.
- **House rules:** where the book leaves gaps, the app proposes a rule and the wiki explains it next to the book's. For example, [parrying with two weapons](../combat/parry-and-two-weapons.md) or [fear by sides](../combat/fear-and-morale.md). Before a game between several players, read them and agree on them.
- **Language:** the selector in the header switches between Spanish and English. Spell and table names are translated; skill names are sometimes also quoted in English, as in the book.
