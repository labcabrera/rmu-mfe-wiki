# From the campaign to the first tactical game

This guide walks through the app in the order a game is set up, with a made-up example: Maela runs the campaign **The Northern March**, Íñigo plays the scout **Arwyn** and Carla the warrior **Bruno**. Their first fight is against a band of raiders.

The rules decisions (stats, power level, development) are in Core Law, chapters 2 to 4. This page explains **where they are done in the app**.

> If this is your first time with the tool, read [what RMU Engine is and how to use it with the books](introduction.md) first.

## 1. Before you start: friends {#friends}

The other players join the campaign as **friends** of the GM. Do this first:

1. **Profile → Friends → Add Friend**, with the other player's exact username.
2. Or **Invitation links → Create link**, and send it. The link works once and expires after 7 days.
3. The other player accepts the invitation from their profile.

In the example, Maela adds Íñigo and Carla.

## 2. The realm {#realm}

The realm is the world of the campaign: which races, languages, regions and religions it has, and how much magic.

- **Core → Realms.** You can use one that ships with the app or create your own with the create button.
- On the realm page, the **Races**, **Languages**, **Regions**, **Religions** and **Historic** tabs show what it contains. The races you pick when creating characters come from here.
- If you want another player to help you prepare it, **Share** gives them read or write access.

Maela uses a realm that ships with the app.

## 3. The campaign {#campaign}

**Campaigns → Campaigns → create**, or the **Start a campaign** button on the home page. The form has five parts:

| Part            | What to decide                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Campaign**    | The name, the realm and the visibility. **Private**: only members see it. **Public**: anyone sees its cover and can ask to join                                                                                         |
| **Calendar**    | The date the story starts. Later, in the campaign, **Pass time** moves it on                                                                                                                                            |
| **Settings**    | How stats are generated (**Rolls** or **Point buy**), the talent policy, starting wealth, lethality, the experience and fatigue multipliers, and the **board scale**, which turns distances into your miniatures' scale |
| **Power level** | A preset (Average, Superior, Heroic, Legendary or Epic) that fills the stat points, boosts and extra development points. The values can be tweaked                                                                      |
| **Lore**        | The description of the campaign                                                                                                                                                                                         |

Each option has an (i) icon with its help and default value. When in doubt, keep the default and change it when you need to: the options can be edited later.

Maela picks **Rolls**, power level **Average** and **Modest** starting wealth, so the characters start with some money.

### The players of the campaign {#members}

In the campaign, the **Players** button opens the member list:

- **Masters:** the owner of the campaign and whoever they appoint. A master can do everything except delete the campaign, change its visibility or manage other masters.
- **Players:** create characters and play them.
- **Spectators:** only watch.

**Add a friend** with their role. If the campaign is public, **join requests** show up here to approve or reject. Maela adds Íñigo and Carla as players.

## 4. Factions {#factions}

A faction is a group that acts together: the players' party, an enemy band, a mercenary company. Every character belongs to a faction.

- In the campaign, **Factions** panel **→ create**: the name and the shared gold.
- In the faction, **Faction players**: who can play its characters. The master adds any player of the campaign.
- The **NPCs** panel adds non-player characters to the faction (see [section 7](#npcs)).
- **Armory:** the faction's market, workshop and enchantments, with its gold.

Maela creates **The Pass Watch** for Íñigo and Carla, and **Moorland Raiders** for the enemies.

## 5. Characters {#characters}

**Campaigns → Characters → create**, or from the faction. Each player creates their own.

### The creation form {#character-form}

A single page with several parts:

1. **Character:** name, race (those of the campaign's realm), profession, realm of power, height, weight and level.
2. **Statistics:** depending on the campaign option, stats are rolled or bought with points. The boosts and swaps the power level allows are applied from here too.
3. **Weapon Development Order:** drag melee, ranged, shield and unarmed into your order of preference (Core Law, chapter 3).
4. **Culture:** the culture ranks, handed out without spending development points, languages included.
5. **Lore:** gender, age and description.

Once the profession is chosen, its development costs and professional skills show up, to keep them in view. Saving opens the sheet.

### The sheet: development and talents {#character-sheet}

The rest is done in the tabs of the sheet:

- **Skills:** spend **development points** (DP) with the rank buttons, add new skills and assign the professional bonuses. The **fighting style** ranks are allocated here too.
- **Traits:** **Add trait**, within the campaign limits (talent policy and maximum cost). Flaws also go here.
- **Magic:** spell lists are learnt as skills. The tab shows up when the character has lists or power points.
- **Combat**, **Health** and **Attributes:** for reference. The app calculates OB, DB, hits and resistances.
- **Progression:** experience. **Level up** shows up when there is enough experience.

Arwyn spends her DP on bow, stalking and perception; Bruno, on sword, shield and armor.

> If you already have the characters on paper, entering them takes a while the first time. A character exported from another campaign is brought in with **Import character**, which first shows what it will create.

## 6. Equipment {#equipment}

Equipment is bought at the **market**, with the character's money or the faction's gold.

1. On the sheet, **Equipment** tab, go to the **Market**. Tabs **Buy**, **Sell** and **History**.
2. Add items from the catalog to the cart and **Check out**: **Direct purchase** at list price or **Haggle**, which asks for a Trading roll.
3. Back in **Equipment**, click a **slot** (main hand, off hand, head, body, arms, legs) and choose the item from the inventory.
4. Check the **encumbrance**: the weight carried and whether it is overloaded. Armor changes DB and the maneuver penalties.

Between characters, **Give** hands over items and **Transfer gold**, money. The **workshop** is for crafting and repairing, and the **treasure tables** for sharing out loot.

Bruno buys a broadsword, a shield and chain mail; Arwyn, a longbow, arrows and rigid leather.

## 7. NPCs and creatures {#npcs}

- **NPCs** is the catalog of non-player characters and creatures. Catalog ones cannot be changed: **Duplicate as my own NPC** makes a copy you can edit. You can also create your own.
- **NPCs → Encounters** has tables to roll encounters, and the result can be **added to a tactical game**.
- To put NPCs in a faction: in the faction, **Add NPC**, with an alias and how many (for example, "Raider" ×4).

Maela adds four raiders and their chief to **Moorland Raiders**.

## 8. The tactical game {#tactical}

The tactical game is the fight. It is created **from the campaign**: in its **Games** panel, the **+** button.

1. **Form:**
   - the name (for example, "Ambush at the ford");
   - the **fear and morale rules**: **house rules** (the default) or **core rules** (see [fear, morale and rally](../combat/fear-and-morale.md)). They cannot be changed once started.
2. **Participants:** choose the **factions** and, for each, the **characters** that take part. The faction's NPCs are added here, or in the **Environment** faction for those that belong to nobody (animals, a passing patrol…).
3. **Sides:** in the **faction × side** table, put together the factions that fight together and, if you like, mark a **leader** per side. Leaving it untouched, each faction is its own side. Sides are fixed once the game starts.
4. **Environment:** temperature and altitude modifiers, if the scene has them.
5. **Play:** starts the first round and opens the **combat dashboard**.

Once started, characters can be added but not removed.

### The first round {#first-round}

On the combat dashboard:

1. **Roll all initiatives** (or type each player's dice) and **Sort by initiative**.
2. Each actor **declares their action** on their initiative turn: move, attack, a maneuver, a spell…
3. **Start phase** moves through the 4 phases of the round and **Start upkeep** closes it, with bleeding, fatigue and warnings.
4. The **Board**, **Log** and **Damage** tabs show the actors, what has happened and the damage done.

How each thing is resolved is in the [Combat](../combat/closing-and-melee.md) section.

## 9. Summary {#checklist}

| Step                                                 | Where                                | Who         |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | ----------- |
| Become friends                                       | Profile → Friends                    | Everyone    |
| Pick or create the realm                             | Core → Realms                        | GM          |
| Create the campaign and set its options              | Campaigns → Campaigns                | GM          |
| Add the players                                      | Campaign → Players                   | GM          |
| Create the factions                                  | Campaign → Factions                  | GM          |
| Create the characters and spend their DP             | Campaigns → Characters and the sheet | Each player |
| Buy and equip                                        | Sheet → Equipment → Market           | Each player |
| Add the NPCs                                         | Faction → NPCs                       | GM          |
| Create the tactical game, its participants and sides | Campaign → Games → +                 | GM          |
| Play the first round                                 | Combat dashboard                     | Everyone    |
